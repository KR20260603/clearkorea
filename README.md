# What ClearKorea Is

![ClearKorea](assets/readme-banner.svg)

> **An online square for citizens demanding electoral transparency and a fair re-vote.**

ClearKorea is a civic platform created in response to the ballot shortage incident during the June 3 local elections. It gives people who cannot attend rallies in person a place to speak up online, support the rallies, and stand together through verified posts.

It is not a partisan site. It is built as a public square for the broadly shared demands of **a full investigation, prevention of recurrence, electoral transparency, and a fair re-vote.**

## Core Features

- **Home** — live counters and the state of the movement at a glance
- **Rallies** — rally info and map, plus a support guide (food deliveries and more)
- **Square** — the core: post your voice in the online protest square
- **Live** — verified YouTube livestreams from the rallies
- **News** — verified statements, citizen posts, and world press (with reporting)

## Getting Started

- Enter from the **Enter** button on the landing page.
- **Guest**: join instantly with no signup (post a voice, comment, report). A nickname is assigned automatically.
- **Sign in**: log in with Google, Kakao, or Naver to unlock extras such as applying to become an admin.
- The interface is a single responsive layout built mobile-first; on desktop the same screen simply spreads wider.

## Layout (Five Bottom Tabs)

### 1. Home
A live dashboard that shows the state of the movement at a glance.
- Two pinned counters at the top: **people who spoke up** and **total voices**.
- A live regional congestion indicator for rally areas, without presenting it as rally headcount.
- Highlight cards for top voices, verified posts, and world press coverage.

### 2. Rallies
Everything about the on-the-ground rallies and how to support them.
- A list of ongoing and upcoming rallies with location, time, and status, plotted on a map.
- A **support guide** (how to send food deliveries and other encouragement) so people who cannot attend can still stand with the crowd.

### 3. Square — the core
The online protest square and the central entry point of the platform.
- Post your voice through the **Speak up** field, and respond with comments and likes.
- The participant and voice counters stay pinned at the top.
- Guests can post immediately; a nickname is assigned automatically.

### 4. Live
A single place to watch rallies as they happen.
- Verified YouTube streams shown in a grid.
- Ended broadcasts are kept available as replays.

### 5. News
Verified statements, citizen posts, and foreign coverage, split into four tabs.
- **All**: a combined timeline of verified and public posts
- **Verified**: posts from public and semi-public figures (semi-public covers celebrities, YouTubers, major influencers, etc.) with a verified badge, embedded SNS posts, and statements
- **Public**: a feed of citizens' encouragement and delivery proof
- **World press**: a collection of foreign news coverage (thumbnail and headline, opening the original article on click)
- The **Report a post** button at the top right lets anyone submit a post.

## Key Features

- **Voice counters**: the number of people who spoke up and the total number of voices are counted separately and shown at the top.
- **Report a post**: submit just a public figure's name and an SNS link. The link is validated, then reviewed by an admin before it appears under Verified.
- **Apply as admin**: a signed-in user submits name, region, contact, a short bio, and reason; the top admin approves it. Unsuitable admins can be removed.
- **Safety**: a report button and admin moderation are built in.

## Operating Principles

- Posting or tracking the personal information of specific individuals is prohibited.
- Encouraging or organizing unlawful acts (physically blocking vote counting, private retaliation, and the like) is prohibited.
- Only lawful expression, sharing rally information, and solidarity through verified posts are supported.
- ClearKorea is operated as a public, open-source platform. Anyone can contribute through GitHub issues and pull requests: https://github.com/KR20260603/clearkorea
- Secrets, admin allowlist emails, API keys, database passwords, and deployment tokens must never be committed.
